# e-Lab Tech

**e-Lab Tech** is the technology and engineering organization behind **e-Lab**, the commercial brand of **ITELAB S.L.**

We design, build and maintain software, automation systems and technical infrastructure that support e-Lab’s operations as a modern technical service company in Madrid.

e-Lab provides electronics repair, computer repair, mobile device repair, data recovery, remote support, PC optimization, solar inverter diagnostics and repair, business IT support, and custom software development for individuals and companies.

## About e-Lab

e-Lab operates as a technical laboratory focused on reliable diagnostics, transparent estimates and efficient repair processes.

Our work combines:

- Electronics repair and technical diagnostics.
- IT support and infrastructure services.
- Custom software development.
- Business process automation.
- Internal systems for repair management, customer operations and service delivery.
- Integrations with ERP, CRM, billing and support platforms.

We build software and systems with a strong focus on:

- Reliability.
- Maintainability.
